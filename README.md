# 💫 About Me:
I am the kind of person who cannot leave a black box alone. When I use a tool or work with a system, I want to know what is actually happening underneath: how the pieces fit together, why a design decision was made, and what breaks when something goes wrong. That curiosity is what pulled me toward cloud infrastructure, distributed systems, and network engineering in the first place.
I am also just stubborn about finishing things. Once I sit down to work on a problem, I do not feel right walking away until it is solved or I have genuinely exhausted every angle. That persistence has served me well when debugging infrastructure issues at 2am or tracing a race condition through an async pipeline.
I am still early in my journey: actively building, actively learning, and always looking to go one level deeper.
